# 0.33.0

_Released 2026-09-28._

A person as a stick figure with a photo for a head, for a team page drawn the
way the foundation's mockups draw people: the digital-gap survey's line bodies,
a cut-out portrait sat on the neck, and the name on a callout. Three components and their
drawing on `./primitives`; nothing existing changed.

[Compare against 0.32.2 on GitHub](https://github.com/fndvit/VizVit-UI/compare/v0.32.2...v0.33.0)

## Added

- **The figure, in parts** (`./primitives`) — a person as a stick figure,
  drawn from the digital-gap survey's body paths.
  - `figure/paths` — the drawing as closed sets (`ARMS`, `LEGS`, `HEADS`, with
    `ArmsPose`, `LegsPose`, `HeadShape`, like `IconName`), where each part sits
    (`FIGURE_VIEWBOX`, `NECK`, `SHOULDER`) and `calloutPath(side, align)`.
    Data and arithmetic, tested in node.
  - `<FigureBody>` — the line body in a pose; `children` render inside its svg,
    in its coordinates.
  - `<FigureHead>` — an svg fragment on the neck: a cut-out photo, a portrait
    masked into a circle, or, with no photo or one that fails to load, a drawn
    outline.
  - `<PersonFigure>` — the composition, plus the name and role over a rule
    joined to the shoulder by a diagonal callout, on either side and at the
    head's or the legs' height; a bio reveals on hover, keyboard focus or tap.
    The drawing is decorative — the name is the text.
- Figure tokens: `--vit-figure-width`, `--vit-figure-ink`,
  `--vit-figure-stroke`, `--vit-figure-callout-ink`, `--vit-figure-name-size`,
  `--vit-figure-role-size`, `--vit-figure-bio-width`.

## Other

- Storybook serves `static/`, and `static/images/placeholders/` gains the
  `portrait.svg` the fixtures had always pointed at and a `cutout.svg` for the
  figure — the card stories showed the placeholder box before this.
