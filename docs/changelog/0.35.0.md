# 0.35.0

_Released 2026-09-30._

The home page opens with the brand's splash and follows it with the timeline
as a scrolly — the three areas of the foundation's work, lab, education and
tools, on a sticky rail beside full-height sections — and everything in it
edits from a CMS: the tagline's emphasis as `**runs**`, each area's text and
state, the wording. `tools` joins the categories so the third area has a
history to link to.

[Compare against 0.34.0 on GitHub](https://github.com/fndvit/VizVit-UI/compare/v0.34.0...v0.35.0)

## Added

- **`SplashHero`** (`./chrome`) — the landing splash: the brand mosaic across
  the top, the «ViT» wordmark over it, the title as a vertical label (the
  page's `<h1>`), the tagline on white with `**strong**` runs, and a
  `hero_scrollHint` mouse hint that stops bouncing under reduced motion.
- **`TimelineAreas`** and **`TimelineArea`** (`./content`) — the home
  timeline as a scrolly: a sticky rail with one node per area (lab,
  education, tools) beside the areas as full-height sections, the node of the
  area on screen `aria-current`. One document for every reader: no JavaScript,
  reduced motion, a narrow viewport and edit mode all read the same DOM top to
  bottom. `TimelineAreaData` is the shape (`./content`, and `./contract` for a
  host's server) — its `images` are `TimelineAreaImage { url, href }`, a
  picture and where it leads — and `TimelineAreaEditMap` the edit slots
  (title and body inline; category and status in the panel; `record` for the
  pencil). Where an area's link goes is the host's (`areaHref`). An area is
  current from the moment its heading reaches the upper third of the screen;
  its node sits at the top row level with the heading and every other node
  waits at the bottom in order, moving as the reader scrolls, and the rail's line draws itself in as the timeline
  rises into view. Each area is a slide: on a wide screen the frame is
  pinned and the areas swap in it as the track scrolls — a swipe with
  resistance: the wheel's travel makes the page yield, ever more slowly, and
  springs back or, past the threshold, moves the cards whole — the current
  up and out, the next in from below — on one curve of ~640 ms,
  from the splash to the first area, area to area and from the last on to
  what follows the timeline (arrow keys and a touch swipe go a slide
  outright); elsewhere they stack in flow, each area's content rising in from the
  bottom and fading out at the top with the scroll. `motion` (`TimelineAreas`) and
  `timelineMotion` (`HomePage`) choose `swipe` or `scroll` for the run, and
  `entry` / `exit` (`timelineEntry` / `timelineExit`) choose it for its two
  ends — from the splash, and on to what follows — separately. A tick runs from the node to the heading, the collage sits beneath the text. The splash scrolls away
  with its mosaic lagging in parallax.
- **`TileMosaic`** and **`ImageCollage`** (`./primitives`) — the deterministic
  seeded mosaic the splash and the scrolly's corners draw, and a one-to-four
  picture composition in the design's placement, whose pictures link where
  their `href` says and, under the pointer, wear a mask naming the project
  they show (`timeline_toProject({ project })`, the picture's `label` in
  bold). **`InlineText`** renders a paragraph's `**strong**` runs
  as real elements and shows the raw source while an inline editor is open on
  it — the `renderInline` / `plainInline` helpers beside `renderBody`.
- **`tools`** joins `MILESTONE_CATEGORIES` (colour `--series-6`, label
  `category_tools`), so «To our tools timeline» is the same filtered history
  as lab's and education's.
- **`BrandMark`** (`./primitives`) — the ViT mark, six strokes at their own
  opacities in `--vit-brand-mark`. `Nav`'s logo is the mark now (the site
  name stays in the home link, visually hidden), and the splash's wordmark
  is the same drawing at a quarter of the viewport. `Nav` is always at the
  top now (`position: sticky`).
- **`PageShell variant="full"`** — edge to edge with no gutter and no top
  rhythm, for a page that opens with the splash.
- **`TileMosaic`'s `focus` and `clear`** — a denser cluster around one cell,
  and rectangles that draw nothing where text goes (the splash measures its
  text boxes after mount, so no tile is ever under a word). Under the pointer
  a tile turns a quarter and takes the next hue, a disc swells, the hatching
  flips, and settles back slowly so a hand leaves a wake (`--vit-tile-turn`,
  `--vit-tile-settle`) — still under `prefers-reduced-motion`. With `arrive`,
  an entrance:
  the tiles travel to their places in waves from the focus; the splash asks
  for it, and its label, mark (stroke by stroke), tagline and hint rise in
  after the tiles.
- **The brand is pink.** `--color-brand` is `#e0005c`, the ViT mark's pink a
  shade deeper so it reads at AA on white and under white text, and
  `--color-brand-light` is `#ff80b5`, the mark at half strength; the teal
  they were stays only as `--series-1`, a dataviz slot that never moves.
  Buttons, the nav's active state, focus rings and every edit affordance
  follow.
- **`--vit-brand-mark`** and **`--vit-splash-offset`** (the nav's height the
  splash subtracts to be one screen tall), beside **`--color-wine`**, the
  mosaic's fourth hue; `timeline_areasLabel`, `timeline_toProject({ project })`,
  `timeline_toArea({ area })`, `timeline_toFull` and `hero_scrollHint` on
  `UiMessages`.

- **`ThemeCollage`** (`./content`) — the weeklies' themes as four labelled
  pictures in the brand's plum, staggered as the design lays them, each a
  link to the theme's weeklies; a `lead` snippet shares its grid. The name
  edits inline, the picture through the panel (`ThemeEditMap`).
  `ThemeData` gains `id: number` and `imageUrl: string | null`.
- **`WordedLink`** (internal, `edit/chrome`) — a link whose two halves are
  wording: a text key and its `*Href` key, typed against `UiMessages`, one
  modal to edit both. The home page, the newsletter band and the comments
  go through it; three copies of the destination helper are gone.
- **The list URL contracts' write halves** (`./contract`): `weekliesHref`
  and `WEEKLIES_PATH`, `transparencyHref`, `TRANSPARENCY_PATH` and
  `MILESTONE_LIST_PARAMS`. Every link to either list — the index's own
  paging, the transparency filters, the home's area and theme links, a host's
  search — builds through them. `createUrlFilters` takes one `href(values)`
  builder in place of `path` + `toQuery`, and no longer exposes `query`.
- **`rail-flow.ts`** beside `slide-scroll.ts` — the stacked timeline's
  arithmetic (node places, area reveal, the run's slide↔area rule) as pure
  functions with tests; `TimelineAreas` writes the rail's geometry as custom
  properties from the same constants its CSS reads. **`watchScroll`**
  (`utils/scroll-progress.ts`) is the one scroll-linked listener, resize
  included, for the splash and the timeline.
- **`CopyIntro role="lede"`**, **`SearchInput shape="pill"`**, and
  `InlineText` taking an `Editable` snippet's `attrs` to decide raw itself.
- **`swipe-driver.ts`** — the stage's state machine (pull, give, spring
  back, commit, keys, touch, the rail's jump) behind a port the component
  fills from `window` and a test fills with numbers and a hand-turned
  clock; `TimelineAreas`' effect is wiring. **The rail's geometry is the
  tokens'** (`--vit-rail-top/step/stack/bottom`): CSS places the nodes by
  them and the JS reads the same values. **`--color-plum`** names the
  design's plum for the theme pictures and the collage mask.
  **`currentPage`** (`utils/paths`) decides the current link once for Nav
  and Footer; **`AUTH_PATHS`** (`./forms`) spells the auth fallbacks once.
- **An area is any section.** `TimelineAreaData.category` is nullable and
  the area has an `href` of its own; `areaDestination` (`timeline/area-link`)
  is the one rule for where «To our … timeline» goes (own href, else the
  category's history, else no link); section ids are the row's
  (`area-<id>`). `TimelineAreas` takes a `collection` and, with it, an add
  slot (the host's form for a new row) and removal on every area;
  `HomePageEdit.areas` names it; `TimelineAreaEditMap` gains `href` and
  `removeOp`.
- **`site.css`** (`./site.css`) — the site's element rules (body text, page
  and section headings, images, focus ring) scoped to `.vit-site`, with
  `:where()` specificity so a component's own size wins. The website's
  global.css and the CMS mirror's restatements had two owners that
  disagreed on who beat `SplashHero`'s h1.
- **Raw only with a caret.** The live `Editable` sets `data-vit-caret` on
  focus; `InlineText` renders the source raw on that, not on
  `contenteditable` (on for as long as edit mode is), so a CMS mirror shows
  `<strong>` runs until the reader clicks into the text. `SplashHero`
  measures its scroll progress from its own box, so a host whose splash is
  not at the top of the page (the mirror) sees it whole.
- **The media reference** (`./contract`): `resolveMediaReference(value,
storageOrigin)` and `foldMediaUrl(value, storageOrigin)` own the Storage
  public-URL shape, the `/media/` prefix (`MEDIA_PREFIX`) and the SVG
  attachment rule; a host passes its origin. Round-trip tested, origin
  slashes normalised, and the fold takes only that origin's bucket.
- **Escape rebuilds the edited element** from the saved value instead of
  writing its text back over the nodes a child's markup owns, so an
  `InlineText`'s runs return after a discarded draft. A `nullable` panel
  select offers «—» in its own list (choosing it saves null) instead of a
  separate clear button; the area's category row relies on it.
- **An external destination opens.** `ArrowLink` (an area's own `https://`
  «Enllaç propi») used to render a link with no href, because it always went
  through the site-path `Link`; it asks `linkDoor` (`utils/paths`) now, the
  collage's rule too: a site path through `Link`, an external URL as a
  `rel="external noopener"` anchor, anything else no destination.
- **`ArrowLink`** (`./primitives`) — the sentence-and-arrow link («To our
  **lab timeline** ⟶»), one primitive for the timeline's areas, the
  full-timeline and weeklies links and the «know more» CTAs.
- **`TileMosaic`'s `hues`** — the palette to draw from; the newsletter band
  asks for navy, the mark's pink and cream.
- **Message keys** `weeklies_go` / `weeklies_goHref`, `footer_name`,
  `newsletter_privacy`; `cta_meetTeam` and `cta_contactUs` carry `**bold**`
  runs now («Coneix el nostre **equip**»). **`know_more_intro`** joins
  `PAGE_COPY_KEYS.home`.

## Changed

- **`HomePage`** renders the splash and the area scrolly where the hero and
  the compact milestones `Timeline` were, and takes `areas: TimelineAreaData[]`
  in place of `milestones`; `HomePageEdit.milestoneFor` is `areaFor`. The
  page is `variant="full"` now, its bands centred at the content measure. The
  closing link reads `timeline_toFull` and keeps `common_seeAllHref` as its
  destination. Below the timeline the page follows the design: the weeklies
  band is the themes as a `ThemeCollage` under its heading, intro and search
  (it takes `themes: ThemeData[]` in place of `weeklies`; `weeklyFor` is
  `themeFor`), closing with «Go to **weeklies** ⟶»; «Want to know more?» has
  a paragraph (`know_more_intro`) and two `ArrowLink`s where the buttons
  were; tile clusters sit at the page's left edge.
- **`Footer`** is on the brand's wine: the `BrandMark` in white with the
  foundation's name (`footer_name`) beside it, the pages as a column at the
  right with Home first and the current page in bold (`url?`).
- **`NewsletterSignup`** is a white card over a mosaic of big tiles; the
  ways in are pills and a privacy line closes it. The flow is unchanged:
  registered readers only.
- **`RichText`** renders `**strong**` runs inside its paragraphs and
  subheadings.

## Removed

- **`milestones_heading`** from `PAGE_COPY_KEYS.home`: the section it headed
  is gone from the page.

**Upgrading:** `HomePage` needs `areas` (the website's new `timeline_areas`
rows, published only, in `sort_order`) and `themes` (the weeklies' themes,
with their pictures), and no longer accepts `milestones` or `weeklies`; a
CMS producer replaces `milestoneFor` with `areaFor` and `weeklyFor` with
`themeFor`. `ThemeData` has `id: number` and `imageUrl: string | null` — a
host's theme schema and query gain both, and a CMS producer takes the id (no
lookup by slug); `WeekliesPageEdit.themeFor` returns the same `ThemeEditMap`
as the home's, the chip reading its `name`. A host that called
`createUrlFilters` passes `href` in place of `path` + `toQuery` and reads no
`query`; `TimelineArea` has no `staged` prop. A host's area rows gain a nullable `category` and an `href`
(the website's `timeline_areas` migration), its zod and its CMS descriptor
follow, and the CMS producer passes `areas: collectionOf('timeline_areas')`
to add and remove sections in place. Both hosts import
`@vit-foundation/ui/site.css` and put `vit-site` on the site's `<body>` (the
CMS on its mirror wrapper) in place of their own element rules. A page-copy producer adds
`know_more_intro` (multiline) to its `home` table and seed. A host catalog
supplies `weeklies_go`, `weeklies_goHref`, `footer_name` and
`newsletter_privacy`, and rewords `cta_meetTeam` / `cta_contactUs` with their
bold runs. `EditableEntity` gains
`'timeline_areas'` — a host with an exhaustive map over it (the CMS's
descriptor and whitelists) adds the entity. A host catalog supplies the four
new keys and `category_tools`; a host's category enum and CHECK derive from
`MILESTONE_CATEGORIES` and so gain `tools`. A page-copy producer drops
`milestones_heading` from its `home` format table and its seed. A theme that
defines its own tokens adds `--color-wine`, `--vit-brand-mark` and
`--vit-splash-offset`; a host whose nav is not 3.75rem tall sets the last.
